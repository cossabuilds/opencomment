Privacy Policy

As of August 17, 2026. This is a translation for convenience. The German version is the legally binding text; if the two ever disagree, the German version governs.

In short

Orbitide is a Chrome extension for comments in circles — small, closed groups you can only join with a code. Nothing happens before you consent. Before you join a circle, the address of the page you're viewing never leaves your device.

What stays on your device

Some data never leaves your device: your pseudonym in the local cache, your consent, your last active circle, and the locally kept lists of which replies and pages you've already seen or marked done. Because that list is fed by your circles' activity data, it can also contain page titles and the display names of other circle members — for example who highlights a page with a star. This stays on your device but is removed when you delete your account.

Your sign-in token is also stored locally, but it's an exception: it is sent to our server on every authenticated request — that's the technically necessary basis of authentication, not an additional data flow beyond what's described here, but it is a transfer, not something that simply stays put.

In addition, a small script runs in the background on every page you visit to compute that page's address — purely locally, with no network traffic. This is technically necessary so the extension knows immediately which page is open when you click its icon. If you'd rather it didn't, you can set Orbitide's site access to "On click" in Chrome's extension settings.

What goes to our server — and when

Before you consent: nothing
No identity is created and no request is sent.
After consent, but without an active circle: no page address
Without an active circle there is nothing to load — the address of the current page does not leave your device. Background notifications (see below) run independently of this, starting as soon as you consent — they only ask what happened in your circles, and simply return nothing without one.
Once you've joined a circle
The address of the page currently shown is sent to our server every time you open the sidebar and every time you navigate while the sidebar stays open — used only as a search term to find comments and reactions for this page, not as a continuously stored browsing history. It travels in the request body, not in the request's address line, so it does not show up in ordinary server access logs.
The page title
Is sent only when you contribute something yourself — a comment or a reaction. Merely viewing a page keeps the title local.
Comments, replies, votes, reports, feedback
Are sent to our server as soon as you submit them, each tied to your pseudonymous identity.

Circles, display names and the join code

Whoever has a circle's join code is a member and can read and write there — nobody else. There is no public commenting. Any member can see and share the plain-text code for their circles; treat it like a key. Your display name applies per circle and is visible to that circle's other members.

Reactions: star and optional mood

A reaction is tied to a page and a circle. The star means "I want to show my circle this" — it is neither a private bookmark nor a rating. After starring, you can optionally add a positive or negative mood. The two parts are visible differently:

  • The star is visible with your display name to members of the same circle, even to people who never commented on the page themselves.
  • The mood stays anonymous: your individual mood never leaves the server, only a total count per circle is shown. Because the star is visible by name, a mood set on exactly one star can be attributed to that one person unambiguously; in very small circles the count may allow conclusions about individual people even with several stars.

Background notifications

So the icon, bell and feed can show you whether someone replied and what's happening in your circles, Orbitide checks in roughly every 30 minutes in the background — only for data from your own circles, never whole page contents. You can turn this off any time under Your data: then no alarm, no request and no badge runs anymore. Opening the sidebar still loads comments as usual.

Where Orbitide doesn't work

Login, account and payment pages, addresses containing an access token, local and internal addresses (such as your router's interface), search engines, social networks and mail inboxes are blocked — the extension never even asks about a page there. This filtering is very good but not complete: a router reachable by a name instead of a number (e.g. fritz.box) is currently not detected.

Deleting and withdrawing

These are two different paths, not synonyms:

Deleting a single comment
It becomes invisible to everyone else and is replaced with a placeholder so replies to it stay readable. The text and page address remain stored on our server and readable to our operations — there is currently no committed timeframe after which they are automatically removed.
Withdrawing consent
Removes only your consent. Your comments, reactions and circle memberships remain in place; the sidebar will ask again the next time you open it.
Deleting your account
The complete path. Permanently removes your profile, your votes, your reactions and your feedback. Your own comments without foreign replies are deleted; your own comments that received replies are anonymized (text and identity removed, a placeholder remains for the reply — that row's page address stays in place). Your own reports remain, without personal reference, for moderation purposes. If you were the last member of a circle, that circle is deleted along with all its content. Locally stored data is removed as well. The underlying technical sign-in with our backend provider is not affected by this — we're still working on that part.

Uninstalling the extension does not delete any of your server-side data. Note your identifier (visible under "Your data") before uninstalling if you may want to request deletion later.

Storage and recipients

Your data is stored with our backend provider Supabase (hosting region Zurich, Switzerland/EU). Supabase is our data processor — not a third party we "share" data with, but the technical service that runs the server on our behalf. We do not pass data on to other third parties, except where legally required.

We know neither your real name, nor your email address, nor your location. Your pseudonymous identifier is created through anonymous sign-in with Supabase — no login required.

Newly created identities are limited in how many comments they can post during their first 24 hours, and several comments in quick succession are slowed down. This is an automatic behavioral rule against automated spam, not a judgment about content.

Your rights

Under Your data in the sidebar you can, at any time, view everything stored under your identifier, withdraw your consent, or delete your account completely — no waiting period, no need to contact us. For anything else, reach us at the email address above.

Changes to this policy

Orbitide is in active, early development. This policy may change, particularly when new features introduce new categories of data. In that case we will inform you separately and, where required, ask for renewed, specific consent — today's consent does not automatically cover future new data categories.
